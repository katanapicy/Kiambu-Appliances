Complete washing machine care

Get Your Laundry Routine Back on Track

A washing machine fault can stem from the drainage system, water inlet, motor, control board, door mechanism or worn moving parts. We inspect the appliance methodically to identify the source before recommending a repair.

Common faults we repair

  • Machine will not start
  • Drum does not spin
  • Water will not drain
  • Machine leaks water
  • Excessive noise or vibration
  • Door will not open or lock
  • Machine does not fill
  • Error codes and cycle faults
Stop using the machine if it is leaking heavily, smells of burning or repeatedly trips the power. Disconnect it safely and contact a technician.
