Reliable drying performance

Restore Heat and Airflow

Dryer performance depends on safe heating, free airflow, drum movement and accurate sensing. We inspect the relevant system for your dryer type before recommending work.

Common faults we repair

  • Dryer does not heat
  • Drum does not turn
  • Clothes remain damp
  • Cycle stops too early
  • Dryer overheats
  • Unusual squealing or rumbling
  • Water container warning
  • Power or control faults
Clean the lint filter after every load. If the dryer smells hot or burnt, stop using it and arrange inspection to reduce fire risk.
