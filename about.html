Who we are

Local Service, Professional Standards

Kiambu Appliance Care is focused on making appliance repair straightforward. We come to your home, assess the problem carefully and explain the available repair before work begins.

Our team works on the household appliances families rely on every day—from washing machines and refrigerators to ovens, dishwashers, microwaves and dryers. We support major brands and approach every visit with care for your appliance and your home.
