We inspect electrical elements, thermostats, switches, sensors, igniters and related controls to find why your cooker or oven is not performing correctly.

Common faults we repair

  • Oven does not heat
  • Uneven cooking temperature
  • Hob plate not working
  • Gas burner will not ignite
  • Oven trips the power
  • Faulty selector or controls
  • Door does not close properly
  • Fan or light not working
If you smell gas, turn off the supply if safe, ventilate the area, avoid electrical switches and contact the appropriate emergency provider before requesting appliance repair.
