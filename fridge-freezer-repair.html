Restore Reliable Cooling

Cooling performance depends on sensors, fans, thermostats, seals, the defrost system and the sealed refrigeration circuit. Our diagnosis checks the complete system so the repair addresses the actual cause.

Common faults we repair

  • Fridge is not cooling
  • Freezer is too warm
  • Heavy frost or ice build-up
  • Water leaking inside or below
  • Unusual humming or clicking
  • Compressor will not run
  • Fan or airflow problems
  • Damaged door seals
Keep doors closed as much as possible when cooling fails. Move perishable food to safe cold storage and arrange diagnosis promptly.
