Clearer kitchen air

Restore Effective Ventilation

An extractor hood needs a working motor, fan, controls and clear airflow path. We inspect the electrical and mechanical components and identify whether the issue is a failed part or restricted extraction.

Common faults we repair

  • Fan does not run
  • Weak extraction
  • Motor is very noisy
  • Speed control not working
  • Hood lights have failed
  • Unit will not power on
  • Fan vibrates excessively
  • Switch or control faults
Regular grease-filter cleaning improves airflow and reduces strain on the motor. Disconnect power before removing or cleaning filters.
