Restore Effective Cleaning

A dishwasher relies on controlled filling, circulation, heating and drainage. We check the relevant pumps, valves, filters, spray systems, sensors and controls to identify the failed stage.

Common faults we repair

  • Water will not drain
  • Dishwasher does not fill
  • Leaks around or below unit
  • Dishes remain dirty
  • Water does not heat
  • Spray arms not working
  • Door will not latch
  • Error codes or stopped cycles
If water is leaking near electrical connections, stop the cycle, isolate power safely and avoid using the dishwasher until it is inspected.
