Get Fast Heating Working Again

Microwaves contain high-voltage components and should only be opened by trained technicians. We assess interlocks, controls, drive components and the heating circuit using appropriate safety procedures.

Common faults we repair

  • Microwave does not heat
  • Unit will not start
  • Sparking during operation
  • Turntable does not rotate
  • Buttons do not respond
  • Door will not latch
  • Unusual buzzing sounds
  • Display or lighting faults
Never remove a microwave cover yourself. Internal components can retain a dangerous electrical charge even after the appliance is unplugged.
